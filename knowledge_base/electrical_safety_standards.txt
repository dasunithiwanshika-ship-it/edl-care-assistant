# ELECTRICITY DISTRIBUTION LANKA (EDL)
## ELECTRICAL SAFETY GUIDELINES & EMERGENCY PROTOCOLS
Source: EDL Public Safety & Operational Health Division
Document ID: EDL-SAF-STD-2026
Category: Electrical Safety & Hazard Prevention

### 1. Mandatory Domestic Safety Measures
Electricity is vital for daily life but carries severe hazards of electrocution, flash burns, and domestic fires if safety standards are neglected.

1. Earth Leakage Circuit Breaker (ELCB / Residual Current Device / Trip Switch):
   - Every home must have a functional 30mA sensitivity ELCB installed at the main consumer unit.
   - Monthly Routine Test: Press the test button labeled "T" or "Test" once every month. If the switch trips instantly, the breaker is healthy. If it does not trip, contact a licensed electrician immediately.

2. Prevention of Electrical Shocks:
   - Never handle electric switches, wall plugs, or appliances with wet hands or while standing bare-foot on damp floors.
   - Never pull appliances by their flexible cords; always grip the insulated plug head.
   - Keep all electrical equipment away from bathtubs, sinks, garden hoses, and swimming pools.
   - Install tamper-resistant safety socket covers in homes with young children.

3. Outdoor & Overhead Power Line Precautions:
   - Maintain a minimum horizontal clearance of at least 3.0 meters (10 feet) between tree branches, building scaffolds, or antennas and EDL high-voltage/low-voltage distribution lines.
   - Do not attempt to prune tree limbs encroaching within 3 meters of overhead power wires yourself. Request free professional clearing from your EDL Depot by calling 1987.
   - Avoid flying kites, erecting flag poles, or maneuvering long metallic pipes near power cables.

### 2. Emergency Breakdown & Fallen Wire Protocols
* Fallen Conductors on the Ground:
  - ALWAYS treat any fallen wire as energized (live) and lethal, even if not sparking or smoking.
  - Maintain a strict perimeter of at least 10 meters (33 feet) away from the wire and wet ground surrounding it.
  - Warn pedestrians and motorists to stay clear.
  - Call the EDL 24-Hour National Emergency Hotline at 1987 immediately.

* Domestic Electrical Fire:
  - Never throw water onto an electrical fire. Water conducts electricity and causes fatal electrocution.
  - Switch off the main circuit breaker at the distribution box if accessible safely.
  - Use a Class C Dry Powder or Carbon Dioxide (CO2) fire extinguisher, or call Emergency Fire Services (110).
