### 1. Classification of Power Disruptions
EDL classifies electricity disruptions into two distinct categories:

1. Planned Maintenance Interruptions:
   - Necessary for preventive maintenance, transformer oil servicing, insulator cleaning, pole replacements, and network reconductoring.
   - Advance Notice: Published on the EDL Care Web Portal and mobile app at least 48 hours prior to commencement.
   - SMS Alerts: Dispatched directly to registered mobile phone numbers in affected feeder zones.

2. Unscheduled Emergency Breakdowns:
   - Caused by unexpected weather events (lightning strikes, heavy monsoon windstorms), fallen tree branches, vehicle collisions with distribution poles, equipment overload, or transformer dielectric failures.
   - Technical breakdown repair crews operate 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, 365 days a year across all provincial depots.

### 2. How to Report a Breakdown Incident
Customers who experience a blackout, localized service drop failure, or electrical hazard can log a report through three official channels:
1. Online Web Dashboard: Submit a ticket via the "Report Breakdown" modal on the EDL Care Web Portal.
2. Mobile App: Instant GPS-tagged breakdown reporting with camera photo attachment of faulty wires or meter damage.
3. 24/7 Toll-Free Hotline: Dial 1987 from any mobile or landline network in Sri Lanka.

### 3. What Information to Provide When Reporting
- 10-digit Customer Electricity Account Number.
- Caller Contact Telephone Number.
- Exact Location / Landmark (House Number, Street Name, Nearest Substation).
- Nature of Breakdown:
  - Single Premises Blackout (Neighboring premises have electricity).
  - Street / Feeder Blackout (Entire neighborhood is dark).
  - Danger Incident: Service wire severed, tree branch on high-tension wire, pole leaning, or transformer smoke/sparking.

### 4. Ticket Lifecycle & Progress Tracking
Upon submission, an automated tracking reference ticket is generated (e.g. #REF-2026-9841).
- PENDING: Ticket registered and queued in the dispatch queue.
- ASSIGNED: Ticket assigned to the nearest Area Engineer Depot and active repair crew dispatched in a utility maintenance vehicle.
- IN_PROGRESS: Technical crew on site conducting line isolation and cable repairs.
- RESOLVED: Power supply energized and verified with local telemetry.
- CLOSED: Incident confirmed restored with customer via SMS confirmation.
