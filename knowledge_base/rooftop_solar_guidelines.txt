### 1. Rooftop Solar Photovoltaic (PV) Grid Schemes
Sri Lankan electricity consumers with suitable rooftop area can connect solar PV systems to the national grid under three statutory commercial schemes:

1. Net-Metering:
   - Generated solar power is consumed on-site first.
   - Surplus solar energy is exported to the EDL grid and credited in kilowatt-hours (kWh).
   - If customer imports more than exported, they pay the difference. If they export more than imported, units roll forward as an energy banking credit for up to 10 years.
   - No monetary payout is made by EDL under Net-Metering; credits offset future electricity units.

2. Net-Accounting:
   - Exported units and imported units are settled financially each month.
   - If solar generation exceeds consumption, EDL pays the customer the difference in cash at the PUCSL-approved fixed feed-in tariff rate per kWh.
   - If consumption exceeds generation, the customer settles the net bill at regular domestic tariff rates.

3. Net-Plus:
   - 100% of generated solar electricity is exported directly to the EDL grid through a dedicated export generation meter.
   - The customer's household consumption is metered independently through a separate standard import meter and billed at normal domestic tariffs.
   - EDL pays the customer directly for every single unit generated at the statutory long-term Power Purchase Agreement (PPA) rate.

### 2. Distribution Transformer Hosting Capacity Quota
To maintain voltage stability and prevent reverse-power transformer overloading:
- Each distribution transformer (e.g. 11kV/400V 160kVA or 250kVA substation) is subject to a strict 80% solar hosting capacity threshold.
- If total solar PV installations already connected to a local transformer reach 80% of its rated kVA, subsequent applications must be wait-listed until EDL executes transformer capacity uprating or network augmentation.
- Customers can verify transformer hosting capacity instantly via the EDL Care "Solar Capacity Checker" by entering their substation code (e.g., TR-402, TR-105).

### 3. Application Prerequisites
- Duly completed EDL Renewable Energy Grid Interconnection Application Form.
- System installation by an SLSEA-accredited solar PV service provider.
- Inverter compliance with IEEE 1547 / IEC 62116 anti-islanding safety disconnect protection.
- Dual-channel bi-directional smart meter installation by authorized EDL technicians.
