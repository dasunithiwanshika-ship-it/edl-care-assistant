### 1. Structure of an EDL Domestic Electricity Statement
Every monthly electricity bill issued by Electricity Distribution Lanka comprises specific itemized accounting components:

1. Account Identification:
   - Account Number: 10-digit unique electricity service identifier (e.g. 0412345678).
   - Customer Name and Registered Premises Address.
   - Tariff Category: Typically D-1 for residential dwellings.
   - Connected Area Engineer Depot and Meter Serial Number.

2. Meter Reading Telemetry:
   - Reading Date: Date on which the meter reader recorded the display or smart meter synchronized.
   - Previous Meter Reading (kWh).
   - Present Meter Reading (kWh).
   - Total Units Consumed = Present Reading minus Previous Reading.
   - Billing Days: Standard monthly cycle spans 28 to 32 calendar days.

3. Financial Charges Breakdown:
   - Energy Charge (Rs.): Tiered unit rate multiplied by the respective kWh allocated to each block.
   - Fixed Monthly Service Charge (Rs.): Grid access, customer servicing, and infrastructure maintenance fee based on total monthly tier.
   - Fuel Adjustment Surcharge (FAS): Fuel cost balancing adjustment if applicable.
   - Adjustments / Rebates: Credits for solar export or authorized corrections.
   - Total Current Billing Month Charges: Sum of energy charge, fixed fee, and levies.

4. Outstanding Balances & Arrears:
   - Previous Balance Brought Forward.
   - Less: Payments Made since the previous billing cycle.
   - Outstanding Arrears: Unpaid balance remaining from preceding months.
   - Total Amount Payable = Current Month Charges + Outstanding Arrears.

5. Payment Deadlines and Penalties:
   - Due Date: Exactly 30 calendar days from the bill issue date.
   - Late Payment Surcharge: An interest charge of 1.5% per month is levied on outstanding arrears exceeding the grace period.
   - Disconnection Warning: Issued if balances remain unsettled for more than 45 days after the due date.

### 2. Digital Payment Methods
Customers can settle their accounts instantly through:
- EDL Care Web Portal (Simulated LankaQR, Debit/Credit Cards).
- EDL Care Mobile Application.
- Online Banking and Automated Standing Orders.
- Post Offices and EDL Commercial Centers island-wide.
